Project Overview & Objectives

Water tank monitoring is a fundamental building block in industrial automation and municipal water distribution. In manual systems, pump mismanagement frequently leads to power waste, dry-run damage, and catastrophic water overflow. This project models an automated, closed-loop liquid elevation regulator in MATLAB/Simulink that continuously compares current reservoir level against setpoint thresholds to dynamically govern pump operation.

  • Virtual Simulation Architecture: Models constant inflow, cross-sectional reservoir dynamics, and non-linear outflow characteristics.
  • Closed-Loop Level Feedback: Continuously senses water height using virtual transducer blocks to adjust control signals.
  • Overflow Detection & Cutoff: Integrated safety thresholding to cut off pump energization instantly when the water reaches critical capacity.

System Architecture & Working Principle

Conventional friction braking converts kinetic energy into wasted thermal heat while causing mechanical wear on brake rotors. This prototype demonstrates an intelligent regenerative braking mechanism:

  • Dual Mechanically Coupled DC Machines: The primary DC motor simulates vehicle momentum and drive torque, while the coupled secondary motor functions as a dynamo/generator.
  • Back-EMF & Kinetic Energy Conversion: Upon triggering the brake signal, the generator converts residual rotational inertia into induced electrical voltage.
  • Voltage Conditioning & Relay Charging Circuit: An intermediate buck-boost regulator stabilizes the fluctuating generator voltage to safely channel current back into battery storage.

System Operation & Logic

Designed to automate precision watering for agriculture and gardening. The system continuously measures soil conductivity and volumetric water content against calibrated thresholds. When soil dryness drops below the threshold, the microcontroller triggers a relay module to energize the submersible pump, delivering exact hydration and automatically stopping once moisture saturation is attained.

  • Threshold-Based Automated Irrigation: Completely eliminates underwatering and overwatering risks through autonomous sensor feedback.
  • Water & Energy Conservation: Minimizes water consumption by up to 50% compared to scheduled or manual watering routines.
